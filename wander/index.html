Wander

Wander is a quiet walking game. You lead a llama named Kumo through the coast redwoods, and every time you play is one day: out in the morning mist, back in the tent by lantern light.

Nothing on the screen tells you what to do. There's no score and nothing to win. The game is whatever you stop to look at.

For the Mudita Kompakt, and any phone with Android 12 or later. Free, offline, no ads.

出たら知らせて

It's a game about stopping

Keep walking and you'll see the path, and not much else. Stop, and the wood gets on with its day around you. A mushroom pushes up where there wasn't one. A slug sets out across the path.

Stay put long enough and the old man sits down too. A while after that he lies back and tips his hat over his eyes. The daylight hardly moves while you rest, so there's never a reason to hurry.

立ち止まるゲーム

歩きつづけていると、見えるのは道くらいです。立ち止まると、森はあなたのまわりで自分の一日を続けます。何もなかったところに、きのこが顔を出す。ナメクジが道を渡りはじめる。

そのままじっとしていると、老人も腰をおろします。しばらくすると寝ころんで、帽子を目の上にのせます。休んでいるあいだ、日の光はほとんど進みません。急ぐ理由は、どこにもありません。

Kumo knows where the mushrooms are

Kumo walks behind you on a lead and can't be hurried. Mostly it grazes. But keep an eye on its ears: they swing toward things you've walked straight past.

When Kumo stops and tugs the lead, turn round. There's usually something under the fern it's looking at.

きのこの場所は、クモが知っている

クモは引き綱の先を、後ろからついてきます。急がせることはできません。たいていは草を食んでいます。でも、耳を見ていてください。あなたが通りすぎてしまったものの方へ、すっと向きます。

クモが立ち止まって綱を引いたら、振り返ってみてください。見ているシダの下に、たいてい何かあります。

A basket, a fire, a cup of tea

The basket only holds so much. Once it's heaped, you leave the rest, and what you leave keeps growing for another day.

Now and then you'll come across an old ring of stones. Hold your thumb on it and he kneels, strikes a light with flint and steel, and blows it into a fire. Hold on the fire and the kettle goes on. After that there's nothing to do but drink the tea.

籠と、焚き火と、一杯のお茶

籠に入る量は決まっています。山盛りになったら、あとは残していきます。残したものは、また別の日のために育ちつづけます。

ときどき、古い石の輪に出会います。そこを指で長押しすると、彼は膝をつき、火打ち石で火花を飛ばし、息を吹きかけて火にします。火を長押しすれば、やかんがかかります。あとはもう、お茶を飲むだけです。

Who lives here

This is the redwood forest of the northern California coast, and everything in it belongs there. A heron fishes the creek. A dipper bobs on its stone. Ravens talk somewhere overhead, and after dark there are glowworms.

You can't catch any of them, or chase them. You watch, and then you leave them be.

The seasons follow your own calendar, turned round if you live south of the equator. If you use Sky, today's weather turns up in the wood as well.

ここに住むもの

ここは北カリフォルニアの海岸のレッドウッドの森で、いるものはみな、この森のものです。サギが小川で魚をとる。カワガラスが石の上でひょこひょこする。どこか頭の上でワタリガラスがしゃべり、暗くなるとツチボタルが光ります。

つかまえることも、追いかけることもできません。眺めて、そっとしておきます。

季節はあなたの暦のとおりで、南半球ならひっくり返ります。空模様を使っていれば、今日の天気も森にやってきます。

One walk a day

A day is about twelve minutes of walking, and as much sitting as you like. When the light goes he unhooks the lantern, and the two of them walk on inside its small circle until it's time to pitch the tent.

That's all for today. Open the game again that night and they're asleep, and the notebook is there to read: it has drawn everything you met. A new morning will be waiting tomorrow.

散歩は一日に一度

一日は、歩いておよそ十二分。座るのは、好きなだけ。日が落ちると彼はランタンをはずし、ふたりはその小さな明かりの輪の中を、テントを張る時間まで歩きます。

今日はそれでおしまいです。その夜にもう一度開くと、ふたりは眠っていて、ノートが読めます。出会ったものが、みんな描いてあります。明日には、新しい朝が待っています。

How to play

  • Tap the path ahead to walk on.
  • Hold near a thing, and he does what it is for.
  • Press and hold till it buzzes, then drag to look around.
  • The notebook is at camp: at night, tap to open it.
  • Walk with someone: at the night camp, hold your two phones back to back.
  • Nothing is lost by going slowly.

That's the whole manual. It also comes as one page for the phone, or larger, to print. Walking with someone needs NFC on in both phones, Wander open on both, and the notebook shut.

遊びかた

  • 道の先をタップすると歩きだします。
  • 何かのそばで長押しすると、それに合ったことをします。
  • 震えるまで押したまま、指を動かすとあたりを見回せます。
  • ノートは野営地にあります。夜にタップすると開きます。
  • 誰かと歩く：夜の野営で、二台の電話の背中を合わせます。
  • ゆっくり行っても、なくすものはありません。

説明書はこれで全部です。電話用の一枚と、印刷用の大きな版（英語）もあります。誰かと歩くには、両方の電話で NFC をオンにし、両方で Wander を開き、ノートを閉じておきます。

Good to know

Phones
Any Android phone from version 12 up, held upright. It was drawn for the Kompakt's E Ink screen, in sixteen greys.
Offline
It never goes online. No ads, no account, nothing collected.
Permissions
Two: vibration, for the small buzz when you press and hold, and NFC, which is only used to walk with someone.
Language
The game has no words, so there's nothing to translate.
Sound
Play it with the sound on. His boots sound different on roots, stone, mud, snow and water.
Battery
While he's sitting, the game keeps the screen awake for up to fifteen minutes so a long rest isn't cut short.
Your notebook
It's part of your phone's backup, and the game saves a copy to Documents/Wander every night.

Who made it

Wander is made by wander, who also makes a shelf of small apps for the Kompakt.

It owes a great deal to Fumito Ueda's ICO, to Book of Travels by Might and Delight, to Superbrothers' Sword & Sworcery, and to Jason Rohrer, whose games let the rules do the talking.

If you have a question, or you found something in the wood and want to tell someone, write to email@wanderthe.dev.
