Wander

An old man and Kumo the llama walk the coast redwoods of northern California, from the morning mist to dusk. Gather mushrooms, light a fire, make tea, make camp. Nothing to win and no words on the screen; the notebook keeps what you met.

Made for the Mudita Kompakt; runs on any phone with Android 12 or later. No internet, no ads, no accounts.

A field note

  • Tap the path ahead to walk on.
  • Hold near a thing, and he does what it is for.
  • Press and hold till it buzzes, then drag to look around.
  • The notebook is at camp: at night, tap to open it.
  • Walk with someone: at the night camp, hold your two phones back to back.
  • Nothing is lost by going slowly.

Walking with someone needs NFC switched on in both phones, Wander open on both, and the notebook shut.
The field guide, one page to keep on the phone; a larger copy prints well.
