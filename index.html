Wander — Coming 13 November 2026— 2026年11月13日に

A walk in the redwoods with Kumo the llama. It takes after Fumito Ueda's ICO, Might and Delight's Book of Travels, Superbrothers' Sword & Sworcery, and the way Jason Rohrer lets a game's rules carry its meaning.

One walk is one day, about twelve minutes by the real clock, from the morning mist to dusk. There is nothing to win and no words on the screen. Gather mushrooms until the basket is full, then leave the rest. Hold on an old fire ring for a fire, and on the fire for tea. Stand still and Kumo grazes, hums, and finds the chanterelles you walked past; its ear turns toward what you would have missed. Wait, and a slug crosses the path, a deer stops to listen, the far wood opens up. The seasons follow the calendar, and the weather comes from Sky when it is on the phone. At dusk the lantern comes on and you make camp. Open the game that night and the tent is there, Kumo asleep beside it, and the notebook has drawn everything you met.

ラマのクモとレッドウッドの森を歩く。手本は上田文人の『ICO』、Might and DelightのBook of Travels、SuperbrothersのSword & Sworcery、そしてゲームの決まりごとに意味を担わせるジェイソン・ローラーのやり方。

一度の散歩は一日。朝の霧から夕暮れまで、本当の時計でおよそ十二分。勝ち負けはなく、画面に言葉もない。籠がいっぱいになるまできのこを集め、あとは残しておく。古い焚き火の跡を長押しすれば火が熾り、火を長押しすればお茶。立ち止まれば、クモは草を食み、ハミングし、見落としたアンズタケを見つける。その耳は、気づかなかったものの方へ向く。待っていれば、ナメクジが道を横切り、鹿が立ち止まって耳をすまし、遠くの森がひらける。季節は暦のとおりに、天気は電話に空模様があればそこから。夕暮れにはランタンが灯り、野営をする。その夜にもう一度開けば、テントがあり、そばでクモが眠っていて、ノートには出会ったものがみな描かれている。

For the Mudita Kompakt

The Kompakt is an Android phone with a black-and-white E Ink screen. Everything here is drawn for it: nothing animates, and every page reads in sunlight. A few settings the phone keeps out of reach, such as the hidden browser and the default apps, can be changed from a computer over USB on Kompakt tweaks.

Each app has a download link, and Obtainium can keep them updated from their GitHub pages. I put out fixes as soon as they are ready, so updates come often. If Obtainium says "rate limit exceeded", give it a free GitHub personal access token in its settings (no permissions needed). To update by hand, download the newer APK and open it: it installs over the old one and keeps your data.

How fast you are going, how high you are, and what the air is doing. Press the speed and it fills the screen. No map, no history, and your position never leaves the phone. Not accurate enough to navigate by.

Star Chart

The sky over you, drawn the way a paper star atlas draws it, black on white: nine thousand stars, the constellations and the planets. Set any moment and any place to see what will be up. Tap anything for its altitude and bearing.

頭上の空を、紙の星図のように白地に黒で描く。九千の星、星座、惑星。好きな時と場所を決めれば、何が昇っているかが見える。どれかを押せば、その高度と方位が出る。

Car Check

What the check-engine light is on for, read through a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter. Each code says whether it is stored or only pending, and clearing them tells you when the car refuses. The codes can go to Calendar as a reminder or into Notes.

エンジン警告灯がなぜ点いているのかを、Bluetooth OBD-IIアダプタで読む。コードごとに記録済みか保留中かを示し、消去を車が拒んだときはそう伝える。コードは暦に予定として、覚書にメモとして残せる。

The phone's own chess app, with a second player added: two people on one board, passing the phone back and forth between moves. Or play the computer at fourteen strengths. A move waits until you confirm it, and can be taken back even after checkmate.

Handheld Games

Game Boy games, drawn in four greys for a screen that has no colour. The picture lands at exactly three times size, so nothing is resampled. Puzzle and turn-based games play well, and Zelda too; a game that scrolls, like Mario, does not.

Tarot

A card for the day, three spreads and a journal of readings, with Pamela Colman Smith's 1909 pictures in greys and a short meaning for each card, upright and reversed. The meanings say what a card is about; they do not predict anything.

One app for Android phones: the capture half of a stop-motion practice. The film is put together elsewhere.
